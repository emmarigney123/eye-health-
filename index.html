BUILT AROUND BOTH EYES

Your eyes do not always behave the same way

When it matters, the assessments separate the right eye (OD), left eye (OS), and both eyes together (OU) so important differences are not lost in one general answer.

EDUCATIONAL, NOT DIAGNOSTIC

A starting point for better questions

These tools can help you notice patterns and understand eye health terminology, but they cannot examine the retina, optic nerve, cornea, eye pressure, or replace professional care.

WHY EYE HEALTH MATTERS

Vision is more than reading an eye chart

Clear vision depends on the cornea, lens, retina, optic nerve, eye movements, tear system, and brain working together. Some eye conditions cause noticeable symptoms, while others may change quietly and are found during an eye examination.

Clarity

How sharply each eye sees at distance and near.

Comfort

Dryness, strain, focusing, and how the eyes work together.

Eye health

The condition of structures such as the cornea, lens, retina, and optic nerve.

Prevention & monitoring

Understanding history and habits that may influence future eye-care needs.

IMPORTANT

Some eye changes should not wait for an online assessment

Sudden vision loss, a new curtain or missing area of vision, severe eye pain, major eye trauma, chemical exposure, or new neurologic-type vision symptoms need prompt professional evaluation.

These assessments are educational screening tools. They do not provide a medical diagnosis, prescribe treatment, or replace a comprehensive examination by an eye-care professional.
